# Welcome to the Fabled Wylds

Welcome to the newest installation in the Cottage Witch series. This guidebook should help as an in-game reference for the mods in the pack. However, we have a lot of mods and a few of head witches to write things, so you may find coverage a little spotty in places while I get some help putting all this info together.

---

## What Even Is This Guide?

First and foremost, it's a reference for things you'd otherwise have to check elsewhere online for, such as where in the world things can be found, what foods tame or breed specific animals, and so on. It compiles data from mod pages and youtube, along with personal experience. I will do my best to keep it current!

Quests will still be a useful source of info, but this guidebook is both searchable and doesn't require me to think of what reward to give you for finding a purple pansy.
